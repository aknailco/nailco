The Coin Sizing Method

  1. i.Rest your hand flat on a plain surface, fingers together.
  2. ii.Place any coin right next to your fingertips, on the same surface.
  3. iii.Take the photo from directly above, in good light. Do both hands, and the thumbs separately.

Send your nail sizing picture with a coin next to your hand on WhatsApp. The coin gives us a known size, so we can measure every nail and shape your set to fit.
